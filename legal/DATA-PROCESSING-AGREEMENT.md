## 1. Subject matter and duration
The Processor processes personal data to provide the BundleBoost App for the
duration of the Controller's use of the App.

## 2. Nature and purpose
Anonymous browsing analytics, bundle suggestions, discount generation and (if
enabled) lead capture, on the Controller's documented instructions.

## 3. Types of personal data
- Anonymous online identifiers (visitor/session IDs) and browsing events.
- Lead email addresses and optional names (only if the lead pop-up is enabled).

## 4. Categories of data subjects
Visitors to and customers of the Controller's store.

## 5. Processor obligations
The Processor shall:
1. process personal data **only on the Controller's documented instructions**;
2. ensure persons authorized to process are bound by **confidentiality**;
3. implement appropriate **technical and organizational security** measures
   (GDPR Art. 32) — see the Privacy Policy §9;
4. engage **sub-processors** only under written terms with equivalent obligations,
   and inform the Controller of changes (current list below);
5. **assist** the Controller in responding to data-subject requests, including via
   Shopify's GDPR webhooks;
6. assist with security, breach notification and DPIAs (Art. 32–36);
7. notify the Controller **without undue delay** after becoming aware of a personal
   data breach;
8. at the Controller's choice, **delete or return** all personal data at the end of
   the service and delete existing copies (browsing data is auto-deleted after
   [90] days; shop data on `shop/redact`);
9. make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for
   **audits** on reasonable notice.
